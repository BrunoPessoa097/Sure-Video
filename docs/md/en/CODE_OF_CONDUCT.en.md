# Code of Conduct
[Português](../../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) | [Español](../es/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)
## Our Commitment

Sure Video aims to build a collaborative, respectful, and inclusive development environment.

We expect all participants to contribute constructively, regardless of professional experience, technical knowledge, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

The following behaviors are expected:

- Respect opinions, experiences, and different perspectives.
- Communicate in a courteous and professional manner.
- Accept constructive criticism and provide feedback respectfully.
- Prioritize well-founded technical discussions.
- Help maintain a healthy and productive environment.
- Respect the work and contributions of others.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors will not be tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or offensive comments.
- Discriminatory or prejudiced language.
- Sharing personal information without permission.
- Behavior that intentionally harms the collaborative environment.
- Any form of threats or stalking.

## Communication Guidelines

Discussions related to the project should remain focused on its objectives.

When participating in Issues, Pull Requests, or other communication channels:

- Provide clear and objective information.
- Avoid personal disputes.
- Support suggestions and criticism with reasoning whenever possible.
- Respect the project's technical decisions and review processes.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are committed to:

- Maintaining a respectful collaborative environment.
- Reviewing reports of violations of this code.
- Taking action proportionate to the severity of each situation.
- Protecting the privacy of everyone involved whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

1. Guidance or a warning.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate contributions.
4. Suspension or termination of participation in the project.

Actions will be determined based on the severity and recurrence of the behavior.

## Reporting Violations

Concerns regarding violations of this code should be reported directly to the project maintainer through a private communication channel.

Avoid publicly exposing personal information or sensitive details related to the report.

## Scope

This code applies to interactions related to Sure Video, including:

- The project repository.
- Issues and Pull Requests.
- Technical discussions.
- Official communication channels.
- Events or activities related to the project.

## Final Notes

This code may be updated as Sure Video and its community evolve.

By participating in the project, everyone is expected to respect these guidelines.
